About

My UI/UX journey started in high school Physics II.

Physics uses math to describe the world, and a lot of it takes intuition to follow. My classmates understood concepts faster when they could see them than when they started from the equations.

How it started

I build interactive products that help people do things more easily.

In Physics II, I made interactive slides about electromagnetism in Microsoft PowerPoint, with animations and step-by-step instructions. I made them for a few friends, and the slides helped them understand the material.

Since then I've worked in creative technology, UI/UX design, human-computer interaction and art.

I'm especially interested in accessibility and usability: making technology enjoyable and usable by everyone, without discrimination.
